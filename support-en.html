Help & contact

A question, a bug, a lesson idea: write to us, a real person answers.

Contact

contact@fretup.fun

We reply in English, French or German, usually within a few days. For a bug, it helps a lot to include: the app version (Profile), your phone model, and what you were doing just before.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. FretUp asks for no account, no sign-up and no email address. You open the app and play.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. There is no FretUp server the app could send your progress to, so we have no access to it. It is also why the app works offline.

How do I back up my data or move to a new phone?

Under Profile, “Export” creates a backup file you can keep or send to yourself. On the new phone, “Import” restores it, replacing what is there. The file is not encrypted: keep it like any personal file.

How do I delete all my data?

Uninstalling the app is enough: everything is local, nothing is left anywhere else. Just remember to delete any backups you exported.

The app doesn't hear my guitar.

In this order:

  • is the microphone permission granted to FretUp in your phone settings;
  • is another app (a call, a recorder) using the microphone right now;
  • is the phone close enough to the guitar, in a not-too-noisy room, and is its case blocking the microphone;
  • does the string ring long enough: let it sound for a second.

Otherwise, “Without guitar” mode swaps the microphone for touch.

The app says my note is wrong when it's right.

First check your tuning with the app's tuner (Tools), and that the tuning chosen in Profile is yours. If everything is right and the app is wrong, write to us with the note, the string and your type of guitar: listening to strummed chords is still being tuned.

Are the chords and songs accurate?

Chord sequences for songs are landmarks for learning, not scores: not all are checked by ear yet. If a chord seems wrong to you, tell us. FretUp contains no full tabs, no lyrics and no song audio; the link buttons open the listening service of your choice (Profile).

Is the app free?

Yes, and everything is open during the beta. If advanced content becomes paid, what you need to learn will stay free.

Reporting a bug, suggesting a lesson

Both by email, at contact@fretup.fun. Lesson and song ideas are read seriously.

Hearing

Play at a reasonable volume with headphones or an amp: the app is no substitute for looking after your ears.
