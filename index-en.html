Learn by playing

01

Lessons that start from your songs

A lesson opens with a riff or a chord change you know, then explains what is going on: the 12 notes, intervals, chords, the pentatonic, the key.

02

The app listens

The tuner, the note games and “Play it” listen to your real guitar. One string, one note, a strummed chord: it tells you what is right and what is missing.

03

A fretboard that plays

Touch a fret and you hear the note. Chords, scales and intervals are always shown on a horizontal neck, just as you see it while playing.

04

Your ear works too

Higher or lower, replay a note, recognise an interval: short games, with no guitar if you like.

05

Challenges against the clock

Chord changes, pentatonic runs, precise bends, a metronome that speeds up. Your records stay on your phone.

06

No pressure

No daily streak: we count by the week. Reviews come back when they should, never to make you feel guilty.

100% on your phone

No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your device, and the microphone records nothing: the sound is analysed on the spot, then forgotten. Export and import your backup in one tap. The app is in English, French and German, with notes as C D E, Do Re Mi or German notation.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes, during the beta everything is open. Some advanced content may become paid later; what you need to learn will stay free.

Do I need a guitar?

It helps, but you don't have to: “Without guitar” mode swaps the microphone for touch, in every game and lesson.

Who is it for?

Self-taught guitarists with 1 to 5 years of playing who find classical theory dull. Electric or acoustic; bass will come later.

Why the microphone?

To check what you play: the pitch of a note, a strummed chord. The sound is analysed on the phone and is neither recorded nor sent.
