2. This website (fretup.fun)

This website uses no cookies, no tracking or analytics tools, and does not measure its audience. No external service (fonts, maps, videos) is embedded that would transfer data to third parties — the font used is hosted locally on this server.

Local storage for the theme — if you pick the light or dark theme, that choice is kept in your browser's local storage (key fretup-theme) so it can be applied again on your next visit. It stays on your device, is transmitted to no one, and cannot be used to recognise you: this is storage strictly necessary to provide the service you explicitly requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG), and therefore requires no consent. You can erase it at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

Hosting — this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit the site, your browser transmits technical access data (server log files) to that host: IP address, date and time of the request, page accessed, browser and operating system used. This processing serves to ensure the reliable and secure delivery of the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); the data is deleted automatically after a short period and we have no access to it ourselves.

Hosting involves a transfer of data to the United States. GitHub covers such transfers with the European Commission's standard contractual clauses; its parent company, Microsoft Corporation, is additionally certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has issued an adequacy decision. GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com.

If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide in order to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

3. The FretUp app

FretUp works entirely locally ("local-first"): there is no server to which the app transmits data. All your information (progress, lessons learned, records, settings) is stored exclusively on your device. As the provider, we have no access to it.

The app requires no account, no sign-up and no email address. It contains no analytics, behavioural tracking, crash-reporting or advertising library, and displays no advertising.

Microphone — the app requests microphone access to listen to your guitar (tuner, note games, chord checking). The sound is analysed in real time on the device to work out the pitch of the notes; it is not recorded to a file, not kept, and not transmitted to anyone. Permission is requested on first use; you can refuse or withdraw it at any time in your phone's settings and then use "Without guitar" mode, which replaces the microphone with touch.

Fonts — until they are bundled with the app, it downloads its fonts (Space Grotesk and Inter) from Google Fonts at first launch (Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA). Google then receives your IP address and technical information about your device. This paragraph will be removed once the fonts are included in the app.

Song links — the buttons next to song titles open the listening service of your choice (YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music or Deezer) in its app or in the browser. We only pass the search or the track identifier; that service then processes your data under its own privacy policy.

Backup — the export function creates a JSON file that you share yourself through your phone's share sheet; the app sends it nowhere. The file is not encrypted: keep it like any personal file. Uninstalling the app is enough to erase all local data.
